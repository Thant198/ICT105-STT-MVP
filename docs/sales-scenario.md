# Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title
Campus Life Hub - Assignment & Project Tracker

## 2. Target User / Customer
- Primary user: ICT Undergraduate Students (1st & 2nd year students managing multiple lab assignments).
- Secondary user: Class Representatives / Group Project Leaders.
- Decision-maker or admin role, if any: ICT Course Instructors & Lab Teaching Assistants.

## 3. Situation / Context
It is mid-semester peak assignment week. An ICT student has 4 overlapping project deadlines across Programming, UI/UX, and Database courses within 3 days. Course materials, submission guidelines, and deadline updates are scattered across different Line chat groups and lecture slides.

## 4. Pain Point
The student spends over 2 hours searching through chat logs to find correct assignment details, risks missing deadlines due to lack of a unified overview, and feels overwhelmed trying to track individual and group submission statuses.

## 5. Product Introduction
The student scans a QR poster outside the ICT computer lab or clicks a link shared in the official class Telegram group, taking them directly to the Campus Life Hub landing page and demo dashboard.

## 6. Value Message
For ICT students who experience overlapping deadlines and scattered assignment details, our Campus Life Hub platform helps them never miss a project submission by centralizing task tracking, real-time status updates, and course filters in one dashboard.

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment
Team Member: "Hey Nico! Overwhelmed with all the lab deadlines this week? Finding assignment links in chat groups taking forever?"  
Student (Nico): "Yeah, I have three projects due on Friday and I keep losing track of what's submitted and what's pending!"  
Team Member: "Check out Campus Life Hub. Just open the dashboard, filter by your course code, and submit or update your assignment statuses in seconds. Your Student ID is automatically masked for privacy too."

## 8. Expected User Action
- Try the demo
- Search records by course code
- Update assignment progress statuses
- Give feedback on usability

## 9. Related Requirements
- FR-03: Student Assignment Submission Form
- FR-06: Real-time Search and Filter by Course Code
- FR-08 & FR-09: Task Status Tracking and Admin Progress Management
- FR-12: Analytics and KPI Overview

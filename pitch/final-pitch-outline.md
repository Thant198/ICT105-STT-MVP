# Final Venture Pitch Outline: Campus Life Hub

## 1. Title & Introduction
- Product Name: Campus Life Hub
- Tagline: Streamline Assignment & Project Tracking for ICT Students
- Presenters: Group STT Team

## 2. Problem Statement & User Evidence
- Problem: ICT students get overwhelmed with overlapping lab deadlines; assignment specs and submission links are lost in scattered chat groups.
- Evidence: 80% of surveyed students spend 2+ hours per week searching through chat logs for course links.

## 3. Target User & Value Proposition
- Target User: 1st & 2nd Year ICT Undergraduate Students & Class Reps.
- Value Proposition: Centralized dashboard to track assignments, search by course code, update submission progress, and protect Student ID privacy.

## 4. Live Prototype Demonstration
- Step 1: Landing Page Core Value Pitch (`FR-01`).
- Step 2: Submit a new assignment entry (`FR-03`).
- Step 3: Filter tasks instantly by Course Code (`FR-06`).
- Step 4: Toggle status from Pending to Submitted (`FR-08`).
- Step 5: Display Student ID Privacy Masking (`6601XXXX`) (`FR-10`).

## 5. User Testing & Validation Results
- Testing Summary: Tested with 5 ICT students.
- Metrics: Achieved a 90%+ Task Completion Rate and 32.3% CTA Conversion Rate.

## 6. Go-to-Market Strategy
- Channels: ICT Telegram Class Chat Groups and Campus QR Posters outside ICT Labs.

## 7. Business Model & Future Roadmap
- Direction: Integrate with university LMS, add automated 24-hour deadline email/push notifications, and develop a dedicated mobile app.
